Clinical Significance of Recall in Cardiovascular Screening

In healthcare diagnostic systems, Sensitivity / Recall takes priority over raw accuracy. Missing a patient who genuinely suffers from coronary artery disease (a False Negative) carries grave clinical prognosis, as the patient remains untreated. Both KNN (90.91% Recall) and SVM (93.94% Recall) ensure that over 9 out of 10 at-risk individuals are reliably flagged for secondary cardiologist consultation and angiography.
